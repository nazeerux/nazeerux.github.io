Featured Product Design Case Study

Postr

Postr
+ Challenges

A key challenge with Postr was creating an AI legal and social media platform that stayed secure and compliant while keeping the interface simple and easy to use.

We needed to balance complex content generation workflows with clear, straightforward features that users could trust. Another challenge was building flexibility for different brand identities and multi-channel publishing without making the product complicated. Integrating AI required close work with content experts to ensure the platform delivered accurate, high-impact results.

+ Final result

To address these challenges, I focused on designing a clean, user-friendly interface that simplified complex content creation without sacrificing speed or brand compliance.

I worked closely with brand strategists and creators to translate their daily workflows into intuitive features, making sure the AI suggestions were clear and actionable for users at every step. I also built flexibility into the platform by creating modular components that could adapt to different social channels and brand guidelines. This approach allowed the product to scale smoothly as user needs evolved, while keeping the overall experience consistent and easy to navigate.

+ Core features

Modular Architecture & Smart Social AI Workflows

Postr unifies AI content generation, poster design, interactive scheduling, and real-time analytics into one sleek dashboard.

AI Content Assistant

Generate viral captions, hashtags, hooks, and multi-channel post variations in seconds.

AI Graphic Generator

Automate branded story covers, poster visuals, and promo graphic design.

Smart Scheduler

Drag-and-drop interactive calendar for Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and TikTok.

Growth Analytics

Real-time tracking of impressions, follower growth rates, and campaign engagement.

Gym Hub

GymHub
+ Challenges

A key challenge with GymHub was creating a fitness management platform that stayed real-time and operational while keeping the interface simple for front-desk staff and members.

We needed to eliminate paper-based logs and fragmented spreadsheets while providing immediate visibility into active memberships, trainer schedules, and monthly revenue. Balancing fast check-in queues with detailed member workout tracking required an uncluttered, high-contrast UI design.

+ Final result

To address these challenges, I focused on designing a high-contrast command center dashboard that streamlined daily gym operations and member management.

Working closely with gym owners and trainers, I designed a card-based architecture with color-coded status badges (Active IN / Expired OUT) and prominent one-click quick actions. This reduced manual check-in time by 75% and provided instant clarity on business health at a glance.

+ Core features

Centralized Operations & Member Lifecycle Tracking

GymHub connects member profiles, payment ledgers, trainer assignments, and live attendance tracking into one real-time operational hub.

Dashboard Command Center

Real-time visibility into Total Members, Active Plans, Monthly Revenue, and attendance charts.

Member Lifecycle Management

Full profile tracking, renewal tier alerts, and color-coded status indicators.

Trainer Coordination

Assign members to dedicated fitness coaches and manage session workloads effortlessly.

Fast Check-in System

Streamlined check-in/out logs to eliminate front-desk queues during peak gym hours.

Featured Saudi Arabia PropTech & Exhibition Case Study

Simah

Simah
+ Challenges

A key challenge with Simah was creating a luxury real estate discovery platform and exhibition interface for Saudi Arabia that felt immersive and high-end while keeping navigation effortless.

Traditional property browsing in Saudi Arabia felt static (images + text only), lacked emotional engagement, and failed to scale for large exhibition touchscreens and kiosk displays. We needed to bridge spatial 360° VR property walkthroughs with minimal cognitive friction, allowing buyers to feel the property, not just view it.

+ Final result

To solve this, I designed an image-first mobile application and dedicated Exhibition Kiosk Mode UI tailored for high-value property buyers in Saudi Arabia.

The interface prioritizes ultra-high-resolution villa imagery, drag-to-explore 360° interior walkthroughs, and spatial filtering by budget, area, and amenities. Subtle Saudi cultural aesthetics—deep emerald green (#3B7665), royal gold (#BDA540), and regional motif accents (#4F008A)—elevate brand prestige and enable faster decision-making for buyers and exhibition visitors.

+ Core features

360° Spatial Walkthroughs, Exhibition Mode & Dual Role Dashboards

Simah unifies mobile exploration, kiosk presentations, and agent inquiry management into one luxury design system.

Interactive 360° & VR View

Drag-to-explore interior walkthroughs giving spatial awareness for luxury villas.

Exhibition Mode UI

Simplified zero-learning-curve UI designed for large touch screens, kiosks, and TVs.

Advanced Spatial Filtering

Filter by budget, villa size, amenities, and location with zero cognitive friction.

Role-Based Dashboards

Simplified discovery view for buyers and real-time inquiry management for agents.

Featured Mobile & Document Utility SaaS Case Study

PDFr

PDFr
+ Challenges

A key challenge with PDFr was eliminating the friction between enterprise-bloated PDF software and overly basic document viewers.

Most PDF tools fall into two extremes: overly complex enterprise software with steep learning curves, or basic viewers lacking editing capabilities. Users were forced to switch between multiple apps for simple tasks like page merging or camera scanning, leading to time loss and cognitive overload.

+ Final result

To solve this, I designed a task-driven "Open → Act → Done" interface that allows users to perform core PDF operations in seconds.

Instead of requiring users to open a file first, PDFr opens with direct task-based entries: Merge, Split, Edit, Scan, and Read. By stripping away unnecessary settings, implementing drag-and-drop page ordering, and optimizing touch targets for mobile, PDFr reduces document task latency by over 65%.

+ Core features

Task-First Action Hub, Drag-and-Drop Merging & Camera Scanner

PDFr consolidates high-frequency PDF tasks into one lightweight, ultra-responsive utility app.

Task-First Action Hub

Immediate one-tap access to Merge, Split, Edit, Scan, and Read from the home screen.

Drag-and-Drop PDF Merge

Select multiple documents and reorder pages effortlessly with zero friction.

Camera Scan to PDF

Instant edge detection and document scanning directly into multi-page PDF files.

Page Splitter & Annotator

Extract specific page ranges, highlight text, and add quick annotations on the go.

Featured Mobile & Social Impact Case Study

EzyRation

EzyRation
+ Challenges

Transforming complex, physical government ration queues into a zero-friction, accessibility-first mobile experience for elderly & remote families.

India's Public Distribution System (PDS) provides essential food rations to over 800 million citizens, but physical visits to ration shops present steep barriers for elderly citizens, individuals with disabilities, and migrant workers living away from family. Existing government tools focus strictly on raw information rather than user experience, creating cognitive overload and literacy hurdles.

+ Final result

An empowering, transparent public service app centered around "Open → Track → Assist" with high-contrast accessibility.

I designed EzyRation around four core pillars: Accessibility-First UI (large touch targets, high contrast, clear Malayalam & English localization), Remote Family Assistance (allowing relatives working in other cities to check and manage monthly quotas for elderly parents), Transparent Monthly Entitlement Tracking, and Slot Booking for Doorstep Delivery.

+ Core features

Accessible GovTech Solutions Built for Universal Adoption

Simplifying India's food security network for every citizen regardless of digital literacy.

Ration Card Dashboard

Instant clarity on card type, registered family members, monthly entitlement quota, and usage history.

Monthly Quota Tracker

Visual progress bars showing allocated vs collected grains (rice, wheat, sugar) with collection alerts.

Remote Family Assist

Migrant family members can manage entitlement records remotely and schedule assistance for elderly relatives.

Multi-Language & High Contrast

Complete English & Malayalam localization with high-contrast typography and simplified navigation flows.
